Hiking and climbing

Hikes to the surrounding summits and huts, over 200 routes on the limestone of the Denti della Vecchia and the Lugano Bike 66.

Hiking

From the baita to Alpe Bolla 3 h, to Villa Luganese 3 h, to the summit of the Fojorina 4 h; from Brè to the baita 4-5 h; from the baita to Capanna San Lucio 4 h and to Capanna Monte Bar 6 h, or 9 h over the summits of the Fojorina and the Gazzirola.

From the archive, 1997: Prealpi ticinesi 5, from the Passo San Jorio to Monte Generoso (in Italian)

Climbing

The Denti della Vecchia are a climbing paradise with over 200 routes on limestone. The Gruppo Scoiattoli guidebook is online at scoiattoli.ch; a printed copy is also kept at the baita for reference.
