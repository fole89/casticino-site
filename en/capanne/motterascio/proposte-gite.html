Suggested trips

Six trips from the hut: Crap la Crusch, Piz Terri, the Greina arch, Plaun la Greina and the Via Crio.

If you have several days, these routes let you experience the magic of the Greina to the full. Almost all of them start and finish at the hut.

Crap la Crusch

An easy walk to the heart of the Greina, via Alpe Motterascio and the Ri di Motterascio.

Route description (in Italian)

Length
6 km
Ascent
+180 m
Time
2 h
Difficulty
T2

Piz Terri

Val Güida, Piz Terri, the Laghet la Greina, Val Canal and the Crap la Crusch.

Ascent

Pizzo Coroi and Capanna Scaletta

Pizzo Coroi, the Greina arch, the meanders, the small rock towers and the sources of the Brenno and the Rhine.

Plaun la Greina and the Terrihütte

The sources of the Rhine, the meanders, the peat bogs, the parallel terraces, the grassy hummocks and the Muot la Greina.

Val Larciolo

A lonely, wild variant off the waymarked paths, reaching the hut from above when coming from the Luzzone, via Alpe Coroi, Alpe Larciolo and Alpe Garzott.
