Courses

Weekend courses led by mountain professionals with experienced instructors: the basics for taking part safely in the section’s activities. The following year’s programme comes out by November; the course documents (PDF) are in Italian.

Summer

Mountaineering

The bridge between hiking and mountaineering: roping up correctly on glaciers and ridges, belaying techniques, rope work when climbing and the use of the various tools. Theory and practice, with map reading, planning, first aid and summit climbs on rock and ice.

Register →
Structure
Seven days in three sessions: three days at Capanna Piansecco in Valle Bedretto for knots, rope, crampons and ice axe; a weekend between the Furka granite and the Rhone glacier; one at the Susten Pass, with a final mountaineering tour. Afterwards you can take part as a second on the rope in tours up to grade PD+/III. Course objectives (PDF, in Italian)
Requirements
Reasonable fitness: 4–5 hours’ walking with a pack of about 10 kg, at 350–400 m of ascent per hour. Hiking experience, no fear of heights, minimum age 16 (with parental consent).
Participants
Online registration from 1 December 2026. Places limited for safety reasons (number to be confirmed); priority in order of registration and to SAC members, then a waiting list. Registration is final once the fee is paid. The presentation evening and outings are compulsory, whatever the weather.
Equipment
Personal equipment is the participant’s responsibility; the SAC lends technical gear to those who don’t have it. The presentation evening covers what you need: best wait for it before buying. Equipment list (PDF, in Italian)
Programme
Presentation evening on 12 March 2027 in Bellinzona; outings on 28–30 May, 12–13 June and 3–4 July 2027. CHF 700 for members, 800 for non-members, 450 for OG youth up to 20 and student members aged 21 to 25; car-sharing travel not included. Programme 2027 (PDF, in Italian)
Winter

Ski touring

To move safely and independently on the section’s trips: skinning up steep slopes, off-piste descents, use of safety equipment, avalanche risk assessment and planning.

Register →
Structure
Seven days: an introductory day in Airolo to check fitness and technique, a theory evening on snow, transceivers and companion rescue, then three weekends at Capanna Piansecco, Hotel Tiefenbach on the Furka and Camona da Maighels, with instruction and tours of up to 800–1200 m of ascent. Course objectives (PDF, in Italian)
Requirements
Ski well on black runs and manage a 1200 m tour with a 5 kg pack in at most 4 hours. Minimum age 16. Also open to snowboarders with a splitboard. Anyone found unsuitable after the introductory day gets 80% of the fee back.
Participants
Maximum 30. Registration from 1 October to 1 December 2026, or until places run out; the fee is due by 10 December. Outings are compulsory, whatever the weather; absences and withdrawals give no right to a refund.
Equipment
Full ski touring equipment. Transceiver, shovel and probe lent on request, included in the fee. Equipment list (PDF, in Italian)
Programme
Presentation on 3 December 2026 (also via Teams); introductory day on 9 January, theory on 12 January, outings on 16–17 January, 20–21 February and 6–7 March 2027. CHF 650 for members, 750 for non-members, 400 for OG youth up to 20 and student members aged 21 to 25; car-sharing travel (about CHF 100) not included. Programme 2027 (PDF, in Italian)
Spring

Climbing

For beginners who want to get into outdoor climbing and for those who want to consolidate their technique: safety, rope work, multi-pitch routes. After the basics, more and more independence under the supervision of a climbing instructor.

Register →
Structure
Seven days over three weekends: first climbs at a crag in Piedmont, multi-pitch routes around Locarno, applied trips in the Central Alps; between May and June, sometimes, climbing evenings and knot practice. By the end you climb independently at crags and on multi-pitch routes: seconding up to 5a, leading up to 4b, with abseiling. Course objectives (PDF, in Italian)
Requirements
No technical prerequisites: the course is designed for beginners. Minimum age 16.
Participants
Maximum 26, in order of registration. A deposit of CHF 300 is due on registration; registration is final with the balance paid at the presentation evening. The evening and outings are compulsory, whatever the weather; absences must be reported to the course leader by the Tuesday before.
Equipment
The SAC lends technical gear to those who don’t have it; the presentation evening covers what you need. Equipment list (PDF, in Italian)
Programme
Presentation on 12 April 2027 at 20:00 at the Trevano vocational school; outings on 1–2 May, 15–17 May and 12–13 June 2027. CHF 600 for members, 650 for non-members, 400 for OG youth up to 20 and student members aged 21 to 25; car-sharing travel (CHF 40) not included. Programme 2027 (PDF, in Italian)
Winter

Off-piste ski technique

For those who struggle on ungroomed slopes: tips and tricks for snow away from the pistes. For members who want to improve, for newcomers to ski touring and for experienced skiers looking for strategies for difficult conditions.

Register →
Structure
Four days to refine technique on different kinds of snow and terrain, read the slope and choose the right tactics for the group, apply risk-reduction measures and consolidate avalanche rescue. Course objectives (PDF, in Italian)
Requirements
The basics of ski touring (skinning, avalanche companion rescue) and a reasonable off-piste technique.
Participants
Number of places and registration conditions to be confirmed.
Equipment
Full off-piste and ski touring equipment, with transceiver, shovel and probe. Equipment list (PDF, in Italian)
Programme
2027 programme in preparation: dates and costs to follow.
Winter

Snowshoeing

An introduction to snowshoeing, in theory and practice: recognising danger signs, assessing avalanche risk and terrain, planning with the tools available, searching for buried people and first aid.

Register →
Structure
Six days: an evening on snow science, a safety day with transceiver, shovel and probe, then two weekends at Capanna Piansecco and Capanna Maighels, covering walking technique, the 3x3 method, navigation and group dynamics. By the end you can go up and down safely on easy terrain, marked and unmarked. Course objectives (PDF, in Italian)
Requirements
Reasonable fitness: outings of 4–5 hours with 500–700 m of ascent. Minimum age 16.
Participants
Maximum 20, in order of registration. Registration is final once the fee is paid at the introductory evening. The evening and outings are compulsory, whatever the weather; the destination may change according to conditions.
Equipment
Personal equipment is checked on the first day. Transceiver, shovel and probe lent to those who need them. Equipment list (PDF, in Italian)
Programme
Introductory evening on Tuesday 15 December 2026 in the Lugano area; snow science on 12 January 2027 in Mezzovico, safety day on 16 January in Airolo, outings on 23–24 January and 13–14 February 2027. CHF 650 for members, 700 for non-members, 375 for OG youth up to 20 and student members aged 21 to 25, travel included. Programme 2027 (PDF, in Italian)

Before you set off

Check the weather and the avalanche bulletin the evening before and on the morning itself, and choose the trip by the conditions, not just the destination.
