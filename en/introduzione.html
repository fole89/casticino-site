The section

Founded in Bellinzona on 11 April 1886, the Ticino Section of the Swiss Alpine Club has almost 3000 members and offers a varied programme for all ages: from the youngest to seniors.

In the mountains,
in every season

Disciplines

Hiking, mountaineering, climbing, ski touring, snowshoeing and ice climbing.

For beginners

Introductory courses in mountaineering, ski touring, snowshoeing and outdoor climbing.

See the courses

Huts

The section owns six huts: Campo Tencia, Cristallina, Adula, Motterascio (Michela), Monte Bar and Baita del Luca.

Beyond sport

It coordinates mountain rescue in the Sottoceneri, works to protect the alpine environment and promotes mountain culture.

How it works

A committee coordinates the various activities, supported by five departments and the volunteer work of the members.

Communication

Trip programme, website, a half-yearly bulletin and the yearbook that tells the story of the section (in Italian).
