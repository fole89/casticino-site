Mountain rescue

The section coordinates mountain rescue in the Sottoceneri, with trained volunteers who work in the mountains alongside Swiss Alpine Rescue and Rega.

In an emergency

Call immediately: say who you are, where you are, what has happened and how many people are involved. Stay reachable by phone.

The rescue
team

Since 1918, when the first mountain rescue stations were set up in Faido, Airolo and Olivone, the section has been part of mountain rescue in Ticino.

Information about the section’s rescue team will be here soon: who is in it, how it is organised, how rescuers are trained and how to join.
